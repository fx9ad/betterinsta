# Privacy Policy

**betterinsta** does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data.

- The extension runs only on `https://www.instagram.com/*`.
- It makes no network requests of its own. The only navigation it performs is sending your current tab to Instagram's own search page for the query you typed.
- It does not read your account, messages, feed or search results.
- It stores one value, whether the search box is open or closed, in the page's `sessionStorage`. This stays in your browser and is cleared when the tab closes.
- There is no analytics, telemetry, advertising or third-party code.

Instagram's own privacy policy applies to what you do on instagram.com.

Questions: open an issue on the project's GitHub repository.
